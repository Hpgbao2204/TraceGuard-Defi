# Historical case studies

The Cream, Euler, bZx, WazirX, and arbitrage pilots are retained as
historical/diagnostic illustrations of replay and mutation behavior. They are
not members of the frozen 20-case RQ5 benchmark, do not contribute to its
denominator, and do not establish benchmark-level causal accuracy.

The authoritative benchmark result is the frozen M4 replay-fidelity package:
the 20-case B2/Geth replay compared with the independent Nethermind producer.
Human causal review remains a separate M5 dependency.

Legacy pilot artifacts remain under `eval/results/legacy/` and the individual
pilot directories. They must be cited with their own provenance and protocol
scope; they must not be merged into the fixed-20 result tables.
